0.20.5

  • Fix: messages often arrived a minute or two late, marked "(1m ago)". If someone dropped out of the fellowship's hidden channel (after a disconnect, for example), everyone else kept sending to the channel only. That person then got messages only from the catch-up that runs every couple of minutes. Now:

- Fellowship rejoins the channel by itself within a minute if you drop out.

- Everyone says "still here" on the channel once a minute. Anyone who goes quiet on it for a few minutes gets messages by whisper instead.

- Someone who leaves the channel or goes offline is switched to whispers straight away.
