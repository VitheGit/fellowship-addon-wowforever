Fellowship

Your own little guild for the friends you actually play with.

Fellowship gives a small group of friends a private chat channel and a shared feed of everything you get up to. Your mounts, epic drops, dungeon clears, level-ups, achievements and (yes) your deaths. And it remembers: if you were offline, open Fellowship and catch up on everything you missed.

No guild needed. No server, no website, no Discord. Just the addon.

Features

💬 Private fellowship chat

  • Type /fs hello! to talk to your fellowship from anywhere
  • Names in class colors, messages in their own color so they never get lost among guild and party chat
  • Send Fellowship to any chat tab you like: right-click a chat tab and tick Show Fellowship

📜 Never miss a thing

  • Every member keeps the full history, so you catch up on what you missed when you log in, even if the person who said it has since logged off
  • A "new since you were last here" line in the window, and a login summary: "While you were away: 12 messages and 3 achievements"

🎁 Wishlists

  • Add the items you're hunting for in the Wishlist tab: shift-click an item in, or drag it from your bags
  • One-time wishes (gear, mounts, recipes): when a fellowship member loots one, you get a ping and a sound. Loot it yourself and it's marked Wish granted!
  • Always wanted (ore, herbs, cloth): stays on your list, with an optional amount (Copper Ore 14/40) and no spam when you loot it yourself
  • Hover any item in the game to see "Wanted by" your friends
  • Reminders when a wanted item shows up in a loot roll, and when you open a mailbox with something a friend wants in your bags

🛡️ Fellowship Achievements

  • Clear a dungeon or raid where everyone in the group is in your fellowship and it's earned for the whole fellowship, with the date and who was there
  • See them all in the Achievements tab: a card for every dungeon and raid, marked Completed with the date or Incomplete

🏆 A shared adventure feed

Posted automatically when it happens: - Achievements and level-ups - New mounts, pets and toys - Loot: each person picks what they care about, from Rare, Epic or Legendary and up - Dungeon and raid completions (once per run, with keystone level and timing) - Deaths, in glorious red, so everyone can pay their respects (or laugh) - Online/offline notices

Every announcement has two switches in the Settings window: share mine and show others'.

👥 Built for friends and their alts

  • Membership is per account: log into an alt or roll a new character and you're automatically in your fellowship, with the same role
  • Members tab shows who's online, which character they're on, their zone and level, and when offline members were last seen
  • Roles: Admin, Inviter and Member. Admin can be passed on, and the new Admin has to accept it
  • Invite by right-clicking a player (portrait, party/raid frame, or a name in chat) and choosing Invite to Fellowship, or with /fship invite Name
  • Works across realms

🛡️ Minimap button

Left-click to open Fellowship, right-click for Settings, and drag it wherever you like.

Getting started

  1. Everyone in your group installs Fellowship.
  2. One person types /fship create <name>.
  3. Invite your friends with right-click → Invite to Fellowship.
  4. Chat with /fs <message>, open the window with /fs.

Commands

Command
/fs <message> Talk to your fellowship
/fs Open the Fellowship window
/fship create <name> Start a fellowship
/fship invite <player> Invite someone
/fship who Who's online
/fship settings Choose what you share and see
/fship promote / demote <player> Give or remove invite rights (Admin)
/fship admin <player> Pass Admin (they must accept)
/fship remove <player> Remove a member (Admin)
/fship tab Show/hide Fellowship in the current chat tab
/fship minimap Show/hide the minimap button
/fship leave Leave the fellowship
/fship help All commands

Good to know

  • Everyone in the group needs Fellowship installed. Messages travel privately between members only.
  • Catch-up works whenever you're online at the same time as any member who has the history. It doesn't have to be the person who posted it.
  • Old chat is trimmed after 3000 messages or 180 days to keep things light.
